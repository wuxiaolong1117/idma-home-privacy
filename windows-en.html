iDma Note Pro for Windows Privacy Policy

简体中文

Updated November 20, 2026. This policy covers the Microsoft Store release of iDma Note Pro (iDmaAI.iDmaNotePro), published by iDma AI. SHANGHAI CREATIVE IDEA ELECTRONIC CO. LTD operates the Home account and cloud services. To contact us about privacy, email manager@idma.cc.

Notes and Bluetooth

You can use the editor offline. The app accesses your handwritten strokes, notes, and images or PDFs you select for import. Bluetooth connects compatible writing devices and transfers handwriting to the app. Local notes and exports are saved on your device. The app sends eligible drafts to your Home cloud space when you sign in and use cloud features.

Home accounts and cloud sync

Home uses your username and password or your email and verification code to sign you in and protect access to your cloud space. The shared identity service at auth.idma.online uses Resend to deliver email codes and processes the email address and information needed for delivery and abuse prevention. Home receives a verified account identifier. Email sign-in and password sign-in use separate account identities.

After you sign in, eligible drafts sync automatically while you are online and on the home screen. Home stores those drafts and their content, file names, timestamps, and file sizes so you can pick up notes on other devices, check cloud storage usage, and recover copies. A Home account can access its own cloud files. Cloud transfers use HTTPS. In the sync settings you can pause automatic sync and check the status of pending changes. The cloud page lets you retrieve copies. Deleting a synchronized note from the library removes it from that account’s cloud space when sync runs.

Usage statistics and location

The Microsoft Store package covered by this policy leaves usage analytics and location collection unavailable. The Home service receives the IP address and request details needed for network connections, operation, and abuse prevention when you use online features.

Service providers and storage

We use the Home service to operate accounts and cloud storage and use the shared email identity service and Resend to verify email sign-ins. These providers receive information needed to perform their services, and processing may take place outside your region. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising tracking.

Cloud files are kept while your account uses the service, until you delete the files or close the account. Limited backups may be retained on separate schedules for recovery, security, or legal obligations. Exported copies stay in the locations you chose until you remove them.

Access, correction, and deletion

To close your Home account, sign in and choose the account deletion option in Account and Security. Account closure deletes the Home account and associated cloud files. Export or retrieve copies you want to keep before confirming. A shared identity account used by other products is managed separately.

Use the cloud page to access synced files and the library to delete synchronized notes. To request access, correction, or deletion, or if you cannot sign in, email manager@idma.cc. Include a way to contact you and the account's sign-in method. Do not send passwords or verification codes. We may need to confirm account ownership.
