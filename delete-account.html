iDma 家庭版：删除账号和数据

运营者：SHANGHAI CREATIVE IDEA ELECTRONIC CO. LTD。适用于 Android com.idmaCN.www 及 iOS/macOS com.idma.www。

在 App 中登录需要删除的账号，进入个人/账号设置，选择“注销账号”，阅读说明并确认。请先取回并备份需要保留的笔记，账号注销会删除 Home 账号、关联登录记录和云端笔记、图片等文件，无法恢复。

无法登录或已卸载 App 时，可发邮件到 manager@idma.cc 请求删除账号或指定云端数据，请说明您使用的登录方式及可联系的账号信息。请勿发送密码或验证码。我们会核验必要的账号归属，再处理删除请求并告知结果。

单独删除云端文件可使用“我的云端”的删除功能。启动地点记录与登录账号分开管理：请在原设备的设置中选择删除服务端地点记录；详细地点最长保留30天。Home 账号注销不会自动注销其他 iDma 产品的统一邮箱身份或您的 Apple、Google、微信账号。为恢复、安全或法定义务保留的有限备份按相应周期清理。完整隐私政策。

Delete your iDma Home account

Sign in to the account you want to remove, open your personal/account settings, choose account closure and confirm after reading the notice. Back up important notes first. Closure removes your Home account, associated sign-in records and cloud notes, images and other files.

If you cannot sign in or have uninstalled the app, email manager@idma.cc with your sign-in method and contactable account information. Do not send passwords or verification codes. We verify ownership and confirm the outcome. You may request deletion of selected cloud data without closing the account.

Delete individual cloud files in My Cloud. Detailed launch locations are managed separately on the original installation through its server-location deletion control and retained for no more than 30 days. Closing Home does not close shared identities used by other iDma products or your provider accounts. Limited recovery, security or legally required backups are cleared on their applicable cycles. Full privacy policy.
