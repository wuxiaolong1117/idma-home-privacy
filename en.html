iDma Home Privacy Policy

Updated November 18, 2026. Applies to iDma Home for Android (com.idmaCN.www) and iDma for iOS (com.idma.www). Operator: SHANGHAI CREATIVE IDEA ELECTRONIC CO. LTD. Contact: manager@idma.cc. 中文

Accounts and sign-in

Account identifiers and credentials support authentication and access to your cloud space. Email codes are sent by the existing auth.idma.online identity service using Resend, which processes your email, code and necessary delivery and abuse-prevention information. Home receives the verified result and stable identity identifier. Apple, Google or WeChat sign-in supplies a verified provider identifier and, depending on authorization, may include an email or nickname. Android offers Google sign-in; Apple sign-in is available on applicable Apple clients. Different sign-in methods do not automatically merge existing accounts.

Notes, images and cloud files

Notes, handwriting, images and PDFs can remain on your device. When you upload, the Home service stores the files and their names, timestamps and sizes to display your cloud counts and usage, retrieve copies and delete files. Normal account interfaces restrict access to your own files. Transfers use HTTPS. Keep backups of important originals; retrieval creates a local copy.

Optional usage and location

Optional usage statistics include first launch and activity, a random installation identifier, app and OS versions, device category and architecture. Network services process the IP necessary for connections; when statistics are enabled it may be used to infer country and city. Statistics do not upload account details, note content, photos or contacts, use a permanent hardware identifier, or support advertising tracking. You can disable statistics in settings.

The separate country-and-city option asks for consent and foreground system location permission. It obtains country and city without sending precise coordinates or street addresses in ordinary statistics. You can disable it, deny permission or select approximate location in system settings.

Launch location history requires separate explicit consent. Once enabled with permission, each cold launch attempts one foreground fix containing time, coordinates, accuracy and the country, city and address available from the system. Approximate permission provides lower precision; unavailable locations are not fabricated. There is no continuous background tracking. Detailed locations are sent separately, encrypted on the server and retained for at most 30 days. Settings let you disable uploads and delete this installation's server location records. Offline deletion requests retry after connectivity returns. Disabling detailed location does not erase existing ordinary aggregate statistics.

Permissions and service providers

Bluetooth connects compatible writing devices. Camera, photo and file access support the respective import, capture or save actions. Location permission supports consented features and Bluetooth discovery where older Android versions require it. This version offers no speech recognition or MyScript and requests no microphone permission. Optional location is not required for notes or cloud features.

Providers include the Home account and file service, analytics.idma.ai, and your selected Apple, Google, WeChat, shared email identity and Resend services. We transmit the information needed for these functions, do not sell personal information, and do not share it for cross-app advertising tracking. Third-party services or email processing may operate outside your country or region; their own privacy policies also apply.

Retention, deletion and choices

Local files remain until deleted or the app is uninstalled. Cloud content remains until you delete it, close your Home account or the applicable service ends. Account closure in settings removes your Home account, associated sign-in records and cloud files. It does not automatically close a shared identity account used by other iDma products or your provider accounts. Detailed locations follow the 30-day maximum and deletion controls above. Ordinary usage information is retained or aggregated as needed to operate the service; disabling it stops new uploads and clears pending events, but does not automatically undo existing aggregates. Limited backups for recovery, security or legal obligations are cleared on their applicable cycles.

Use in-app deletion, account closure and location/statistics controls to manage your choices, or contact manager@idma.cc for access, correction or deletion. We verify account ownership as needed to protect other users. A parent or guardian should guide children's use and decisions about cloud and location features. Material changes will be reflected here, with renewed consent when required.
